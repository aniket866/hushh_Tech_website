## The Options Engine: Turning Volatility Into Income

Here's the secret the Absolute 27 unlocks: systematic options income without speculation.

We don't "trade" options. We run a rules-based engine that exploits two mathematical certainties:

#### 1. Mean Reversion in Volatility

When great companies spike on euphoria (RSI > 70, trading 2+ standard deviations above the mean), implied volatility expands. Option premiums become expensive.

We sell covered calls at 5-10% out-of-the-money strikes, collecting 1-3% premiums for 30-45 days of exposure.

#### 2. Panic Creates Opportunity

When markets crash and great companies sell off (RSI < 30, trading 2+ standard deviations below the mean), we want to own more.

We sell cash-secured puts at strikes where we'd happily buy shares, collecting premium while waiting for assignment.

## The Options Protocol: Systematic, Not Speculative

#### Trigger Conditions

RSI(14) crosses 70 (calls) or 30 (puts), stock moves 2+ standard deviations, implied volatility rank > 50th percentile, no earnings within 14 days

#### Strike Selection

5-10% out-of-the-money, preferring round numbers ($100, $150, $200) for maximum liquidity

#### Expiration Window

30-45 days to expiration (DTE) – the "theta sweet spot" where time decay accelerates

#### Position Sizing

Never more than 50% of a holding per cycle, maximum 25-35% total portfolio options exposure

#### Roll Rules

Close at 50% profit or 21 DTE, roll up/out if breached, stand down during earnings

These rules remove emotion. The system runs itself, generating 15-25% annual income on the equity sleeve – on top of the 12-18% organic business growth.

Every basis point of fee drag compounds against you for decades. On a $100 million portfolio, a 0.50% fee costs $500,000 annually – $15 million over 30 years before compounding.

We treat vehicle selection with the same rigor we apply to security selection. Every holding must pass our five-factor scorecard:

#### Expense Ratio (40% weight)

Target: Sub-5 basis points for core holdings. Acceptable: Up to 20 bps for specialized exposures. Avoid: Anything above 50 bps without extraordinary justification.

Average daily volume above $100M minimum. Prefer $500M+ for seamless execution at scale. Tight bid-ask spreads (< 0.05%) for institutional-size trades.

Below 0.50% annually for index-tracking vehicles. We want pure beta, not active management hidden in a passive wrapper.

#### Tax Efficiency (15% weight)

ETF structure strongly preferred (in-kind redemption mechanism). Mutual funds acceptable only in tax-advantaged accounts. Avoid K-1 issuers for taxable portfolios.

#### Platform Access (10% weight)

Available on all major custodians (Schwab, Fidelity, Interactive Brokers, prime brokers). No proprietary vehicles requiring special accounts.

## Tax Location: The Hidden Alpha Source

Sophisticated investors don't just choose the right assets. They choose the right account for each asset.

The US tax code is extraordinarily complex, but it creates massive alpha opportunities for those who understand asset location. The basic principle:

> "Put tax-inefficient assets in tax-advantaged accounts. Put tax-efficient assets in taxable accounts where they can compound with minimal drag."

For a $50 million portfolio, proper tax location can add 0.50-1.00% annual alpha – $25-50 million in cumulative wealth over three decades.

## Tax Location Decision Tree

#### Tax Character

Identify each asset's tax treatment: qualified dividends, ordinary income, capital gains, collectibles

#### Marginal Rate

Calculate tax rate differential: 37% ordinary vs. 20% LTCG vs. 28% collectibles vs. 0% in IRA

#### Optimal Location

High-rate items → tax-advantaged. Low-rate items → taxable. Maintain asset allocation across accounts.

## Risk Disclosure: What Can Go Wrong

Every investment strategy carries risks. Transparency demands we discuss them openly:

#### Equity Concentration Risk

27 names is concentrated vs. 500-stock indices. If several Tier 1 companies underperform simultaneously, portfolio returns suffer. Mitigation: Choose only the highest-quality FCF growers with durable moats.

#### Options Assignment Risk

Covered calls may be assigned, forcing sale of appreciated shares. Cash-secured puts may be assigned, requiring purchase at higher-than-market prices. Mitigation: Rules-based system limits exposure, accepts assignments as part of strategy.

#### Interest Rate Risk

Rising rates hurt bond values. TIPS, aggregate bonds, and REITs all correlate negatively with rate increases. Mitigation: Short-duration bias, diversification across asset classes.

#### Inflation Risk

Unexpected inflation erodes purchasing power. While TIPS, commodities, and gold provide protection, severe stagflation (1970s-style) challenges all portfolios. Mitigation: Real asset allocation (20-30% of portfolio).

#### Geopolitical Risk

Global companies face regulatory, currency, and political risks. Aramco specifically carries Middle East geopolitical risk. Mitigation: Geographic diversification, flexibility to substitute exposures.
